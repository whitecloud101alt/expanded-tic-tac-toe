The rules

Make space. Make your move.

  1. 01

    Start with a classic empty 3 × 3 grid.

  2. 02

    On your turn, either place your mark or expand the board.

  3. 03

    An expansion adds exactly four empty squares. Each must touch the board or another new square by an edge or corner.

  4. 04

    Or place one blocker on an empty square or in a new square touching the current board. This move adds no regular squares.

  5. 05

    The first player with four marks in a row in any direction wins.
